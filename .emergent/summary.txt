The user wants to build a mobile app for a smash repairs business to manage the cars that come in and out for work.

The requested features evolved during the conversation to include:
- User authentication (JWT-based).
- Comprehensive car and job details, including photos, insurance info, and cost estimates.
- A detailed job status flow.
- AI-powered scanning of vehicle registration plates to auto-fill vehicle details (make, model, year, color, and registration number).
- A Quick Create feature to create a job with only vehicle details, allowing other information to be added later.
- A Returning Customer feature to auto-fill owner details from past jobs based on the vehicle registration.
- A contact list to manage all client details.
- An invoicing section to create and manage invoices for jobs, with a future goal of integrating with Xero.
